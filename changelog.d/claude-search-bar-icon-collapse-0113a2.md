### Changed
- **The phone's bottom sheet is gone**: every mobile panel was a draggable sheet with peek/half/full snap points, which meant the bottom of the screen was permanently spoken for. The map was whatever was left over, every control docked down there had to be pushed clear of a height that changed under the finger, and each panel body had to be authored twice — once for the single row a peek shows and once for the rest. What replaces it is shaped by what each panel is for: the settings drop from the top edge as a rounded glass panel, sized to what they hold, so the map stays lit underneath while you change what it draws; a station, a port or a webcam takes the screen, because it is the thing you asked for and there is nothing behind it to keep. Filters open from a launcher button on the map and close from the panel's own title bar. Held sideways the panels are still side rails, and desktop still has both.
- **The phone's search bar rests as its icon**: the floating field ran the full width of the screen across the top of the map whether or not anyone was searching — a band of chrome over the one thing the app is for, and empty of anything readable until you typed in it. It now sits as a single round button in the corner, unfolds into the full field when tapped, and folds back when left empty (Escape, or the icon again, closes it early). A standing query keeps it open, since the results underneath it are the point.
- **Selecting a ship keeps the map**: tapping a vessel handed the screen to a page describing it, which is the one thing a map cannot spare — you tapped a marker to see where it is. What comes up now is a bar in the map's top strip, in the gap between the search button and the settings and filter ones: the ship's name, its speed and its course, and nothing else a glance doesn't need. Tapping it unfolds it downward, in the same column, into the rest — what the ship is doing, where it is headed and when it arrives, the follow and track-history controls, and its identity behind one more fold. Tapping the same row folds it back: one row, one gesture, both directions. It stops well short of the bottom, so the docked transport and a band of map stay under it, the search and filter buttons stay reachable on either side, and everything the fold holds fits without the panel scrolling. The bar's own X drops the selection.
- **The map's own controls stop moving**: the locate button, the attribution and the fleet-replay bar were rebased on the active sheet's live height, so they slid up and down with every drag. With nothing anchored to the bottom edge any more they keep a fixed offset above the tab bar.
- **The filter page's title is back on phones**: the sheet hid it because the one visible row was too expensive to spend on a name the tab bar already carried. A page has room, and the title bar is what carries the way out.

### Added
- **Search opens with the nearest**: the "Nearest" list was a section inside the settings panel, which is the wrong place for it — reaching it meant covering the map with a panel and scrolling past the filters, and by then you had lost the thing you were pointing at. It is what the search box offers the moment it unfolds, before anything is typed: the five ships or trains closest to the middle of the map, each with its distance, one tap away. Typing replaces them with matches as before. The map's opening view seeds it, so the offer is there on the first tap rather than only after something has panned the map.
- **Filtering without leaving the map**: hiding a ship category or a train type meant opening the settings page, scrolling to the list, tapping, and closing the page again — with the map gone for the whole of it, which is the one thing you need to see to know whether the filter did what you wanted. A button under the settings launcher now shows the mode's kinds as small pills down the right edge of the map — ship categories, train types, road layers, each with its marker's colour or glyph and its live count — and tapping one takes effect under it. A dot on the button says when what you are looking at is a subset, and a "Show all" pill puts everything back. The phone's settings page no longer lists them; the desktop rail still does, and both drive the same state.
- **Raide and Tie search from the map**: only Meri had search on the map; the other two kept theirs inside the panel, which meant opening the filters to look for a train. All three now carry the same folded search button over the map, with the box moving back into the rail on desktop and in landscape.
- **A docked transport for a ship's recorded track**: with track history on, the phone gets the replay controls across the bottom of the screen, resting on the tab bar. The scrubber is the control you actually drag, and it now runs the full width instead of the slot left over inside a card. Desktop keeps unfolding it in the top card, where there is room beside the ship's name.

### Removed
- **The draggable sheet, and everything that served it**: snap points and drag gestures, the per-view resting heights, the sheet heights published to CSS for the map controls to dodge, and the camera padding that re-framed the map into the band a sheet left visible. A page needs none of it — while it is up there is no map to re-frame, and while it is down there is nothing to dodge.
