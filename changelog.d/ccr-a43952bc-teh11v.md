### Added
- **Ilma, a fourth mode for air traffic**: every aircraft over Finland on the same live map as ships, trains and roads, with its own tab in the mode switcher and the phone's dock. Digitraffic has no open feed for Finnish airspace, so positions come from community ADS-B networks instead: [adsb.fi](https://adsb.fi), run from Finland and so densest exactly here, with [adsb.lol](https://adsb.lol) taking over whenever adsb.fi fails. The backend polls them every 10 s as two 250 NM circles that together reach from Hanko to Utsjoki, merges the overlap, and drops surface vehicles, stale positions and anything well outside Finland.
- **Aircraft drawn as they fly**: each aircraft is a top-down silhouette turned to its track, coloured and shaped by kind (airline flights, general aviation, helicopters, military and state), with its callsign from regional zoom and its flight level once zoomed in. Between polls the map projects every aircraft forward along its track, so a jet glides across the map instead of jumping two kilometres every ten seconds. Aircraft on the ground fade back, and a 7500/7600/7700 squawk or a transponder emergency gets a red ring.
- **Flight details and trails**: selecting an aircraft draws its last 30 minutes, coloured by altitude so a climb-out or an approach reads at a glance, and shows altitude in feet and metres, ground speed in knots and km/h, vertical rate, track, type, registration, operator, squawk and whether the position came from ADS-B or multilateration. Search finds flights by callsign, registration, type, airline or ICAO address, and offers the aircraft nearest the middle of the map before anything is typed.
- **Finnish airports**: the scheduled airports and the main airfields are on the map; selecting one lists the traffic on its ground or low overhead within 25 km — arrivals on final, departures climbing out, aircraft taxiing.

### Notes
- New endpoints `/api/ilma/aircraft`, `/api/ilma/aircraft/{hex}/trail` and `/api/ilma/airports` (see docs/API.md), and `/api/health` reports `modes.ilma` with `active_aircraft`, `aircraft_poll_age_sec`, `source` and `trails`. No new configuration or keys: both networks are keyless. adsb.fi's open data is for personal, non-commercial use, and both networks are credited in the map attribution.
