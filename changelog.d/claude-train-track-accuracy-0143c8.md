### Added
- **Raide station names on the map**: stations used to be bare pins and dots, with the name only in a hover popup, which didn't help on a phone at all. Full stations are now labelled from the opening country-wide view, and smaller stopping points get their names from zoom 10, once there is room. Each label moves to whichever side of its marker is free, and the names of full stations take priority over the moving train-number labels, so busy hubs like Helsinki and Pasila stay readable. Clicking a name opens that station's departure board, the same as clicking its marker, and the Stations toggle hides the names along with the markers.
